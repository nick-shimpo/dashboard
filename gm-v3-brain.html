Mind palace · fragments.

Carcinid · sound

  • A clicking that pauses when you pause, then resumes half a beat later.
  • Wet chitin sliding across metal.
  • Breathing in a pattern that isn't breathing. Four short, one long.
  • The Shriek — half insect cry, half imitation of a human voice.
  • Silence. They have stopped. They are listening.

Carcinid · movement

  • Sideways faster than forwards.
  • Four limbs working together, two against — the body hasn't decided.
  • Pauses mid-stride, considers, changes direction.
  • Turns its whole front section toward a wall where there is nothing.

Carcinid · uncanny

  • Its eye moves independently. It's looking past you.
  • A scrap of marine webbing fused to its carapace.
  • The body doesn't stop moving for ten seconds after it dies.
  • Still skin underneath the chitin. Human skin.

Station · stairwell

  • Emergency lighting flickers when the reactor does.
  • Clicks echo. You can't tell which floor they're on.
  • Blood smear on a handrail, two flights long.
  • A door welded shut from the inside.
  • The number painted on the landing is wrong.

Station · reactor corridor

  • Walls still warm. Metal ticks — a sound like many small things walking.
  • A service robot, powered on, head turning slowly. Won't respond.
  • Wall murals — the sunset is the wrong colour. No one painted over it.
  • The reactor's pulse — a low biological thump. It was not biological three days ago.

Outdoors · jungle storm

  • Rain in horizontal sheets, hitting the station like gunfire.
  • Jungle wildlife is silent. Has been for hours.
  • Lightning horizontal, across the ridge, for two seconds.
  • The mud is warm. The ground is putting out heat.
  • A palm-sized beetle on your shoulder. It does not fly.

Shriek · control signal

  • You feel it before you hear it. A pressure. Then the sound.
  • Every carcinid stops. They are listening. Not to you.
  • It's coming from inside the mountain.
  • Lights dim — not flicker. As if something drew power through them.
  • Plants lean toward the mountain for several seconds afterwards.

Transformation · stages

  • 1 · Contact — patches of plant growth where there shouldn't be any.
  • 2 · Integration — a beetle twice its size, moving without its swarm.
  • 3 · Symbiosis — a marine body cocooned, breathing in rhythm with the mother.
  • 4 · Directed — the Mimic.
  • 5 · Integrated — reserved for the alien ship.

Horror telescope

  • The mother's punctures match the mountain ridge line.
  • Carcinids in circle-arrangements face the mountain. All of them.
  • The storm is a spiral. Centred on the mountain.
  • Long-range comms: eight-symbol pattern everywhere. The ground is broadcasting.
  • Even the moon has unusual spectral readings.

Hinton · voice

  • "They don't think like us. They don't have to."
  • "They've done this a thousand times."
  • "They don't destroy. They include. That's the terrifying part."
  • "I'm going to be the first human who chooses it."

Maas · transformation

  • 1 · Quieter. Stays in her office. Murals retouched — sunset wrong colour.
  • 2 · Long sleeves even in heat. A patch of skin. She sees you notice.
  • 3 · She asks: "Why are you afraid of this?"

Endgame · set pieces

  • The ship awakes — wildlife flees, storm reorients, mountain sheds.
  • Voice of the elders — presence in thought. Not hostile. Informational.
  • Complete — atmosphere changes colour, crater the size of a continent.
  • Interrupted — process stops but something is still in the mountain, dormant.
