💧 Spillway

Unsettling: Flooded with torrential stormwater. Ceiling maintenance ladder is the only route. Everything coated in sticky organic slick — wrong texture, clings to hands.
Useful: Bypasses carcinid-choked corridors above. Alternate route down.
Dangerous: Aquatic-adapted carcinid in the water. Human DNA — swims with legs instead of scuttling with claws. Moves wrong.
SENSORY: Tactile/Visual. Slick on skin, water, shape in the murk.

🔊 Stairwell

Unsettling: Vertical echo chamber. Clicking sounds funnel from above and below — impossible to locate source. Every landing is a blind corner.
Useful: Vertical access between station levels. Direct route.
Dangerous: Ambush geometry + echoing clicks. BUT NO ACTUAL ENCOUNTER. Psychological threat only. Willis's journey is DREAD, not combat.
SENSORY: Audio. Echoes, clicks, anticipation of what's around each corner.

🌧 Outdoors / Jungle

Unsettling: Dead silence. No wildlife, no insects — just storm and water. The jungle is holding its breath.
Useful: Alternative route between buildings. Connects to chimney (Alpha) or steep path to dam base. Willis's escape valve.
Dangerous: Terrain: steep, muddy, zero visibility. One slip on the dam path and you're falling. No carcinids — just gravity and rain.
SENSORY: Absence. Silence where there should be life. Blindness. Isolation.

☢ Reactor (Mother's Chamber)

Unsettling: Warm, humid air that smells organic and sweet. Cocoons visible in flashlight beams. Marines in the walls — still alive, slowly changing. The mother is fused with infrastructure. A factory, not a creature.
Useful: Emergency med unit here. Franco is here (Hinton reveal). Reactor controls.
Dangerous: The horror is WITNESSING THE PROCESS. No boss fight. Crabsong escalation in reserve if they linger.
THIS IS THE PAYOFF. Everything before builds to this reveal.
