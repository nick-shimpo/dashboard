GM screen · prototypes

Three design directions for the desktop-first GM screen. Same content, radically different philosophy for how it's accessed during play. Pick one to develop, or combine what works.

PROTOTYPE 01

Cockpit

everything visible, search everything

A dense ops dashboard. Nothing is hidden. Perils live down the left rail, party down the right, scene-dressing rollers in the centre, truths as a permanent top strip. ⌘K opens a search palette over everything.

  • Dark, monospace-heavy, mission-control aesthetic
  • Perils as live cards with omen pips and state colour
  • Command palette searches perils, entities, lore, rules
  • Best for: players who like things where they last saw them
OPEN →best for dense tracking
PROTOTYPE 02

Stage

one mode at a time, beautifully

The screen physically transforms between RESET, LOOP, IMPROV and COMBAT modes. Light, editorial, serif-led typography. In IMPROV mode a single fragment takes the centre of the screen — because that's all you need to read aloud.

  • Light, warm paper palette · Cormorant serif
  • Mode switch is the star of the header
  • IMPROV is a single big fragment — cinematic
  • Best for: GMs who want to be inside one thought at a time
OPEN →best for pacing + focus
PROTOTYPE 03

Campaign Brain

a graph of the whole campaign

The campaign as a node graph — entities, perils, locations, PCs, and the central mystery (the ship) connected by lines of relationship. Hot threads glow red. A drawer opens rich detail. A "mind palace" tab surfaces the improv libraries.

  • Dark cyberpunk · glowing connections · spatial memory
  • Ship is the centre; everything orbits it
  • Libraries live in a separate "mind palace" view
  • Best for: pre-session thinking + zoom-out moments
OPEN →best for spatial thinkers
